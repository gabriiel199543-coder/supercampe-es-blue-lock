# Manager Loop

This prototype should feel like coaching a strange, funny, high-drama version of
Football Manager.

## Primary Fantasy

The player is not controlling every touch. The player is the coach responsible for
turning chaotic historical and internet figures into a functioning football team.

## Turn Structure

1. Read the squad.
2. Choose the training focus.
3. Pick a focus player.
4. Set formation and identity.
5. Give the team talk.
6. Simulate the match in 15-minute blocks.
7. React to assistant reports.
8. Watch morale, fatigue, pressure, and awakening change.
9. Repeat across fixtures.

## Tactical Identities

- Control: favors vision, technique, discipline, and midfielders.
- Vertical: favors physique, finishing, tempo, and direct attackers.
- Compact: favors defense, discipline, emotional control, and lower risk.
- Ego: favors ego and creativity, but increases volatility.

## Player Development

Players evolve through the kind of football they repeatedly succeed at:

- dribble wins push dribble evolution
- finishing wins push striker evolution
- pass wins push creator evolution
- sprint wins push runner evolution
- defensive stops push defender evolution
- special actions push ego evolution

## Design Rule

The match should be readable as a consequence of managerial choices. If the player
loses, they should have a plausible coaching question to ask next:

- Did I train the wrong thing?
- Did I overuse pressing?
- Did I give too much freedom?
- Did I pick the wrong focus player?
- Did I release ego before the squad had discipline?
